# AROH Foundation
The AROH Foundation is a non-governmental organization (NGO) based in India that works towards the socio-economic development of underprivileged communities. Established with the aim of empowering marginalized sections of society, AROH focuses on education, healthcare, and livelihood generation. Through its initiatives, the foundation strives to create a positive impact on the lives of the disadvantaged, promoting equality and social justice.

## Key Details
AROH Foundation was founded in 2001 and is registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860. The foundation operates in multiple states across India, with a strong presence in rural and urban areas. It has implemented various projects in collaboration with government agencies, corporate entities, and other NGOs, leveraging resources and expertise to maximize its reach and impact. AROH is also registered under Section 12A and 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961, making donations to the foundation eligible for tax exemption.

## Who Needs This
The AROH Foundation's initiatives and services are designed to benefit underprivileged communities, including rural households, marginalized groups, and disadvantaged children. NGOs and companies operating in the development sector, particularly those focused on education, healthcare, and livelihood generation, may find it beneficial to collaborate with AROH Foundation. Corporate entities looking to undertake corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives can also partner with AROH to support its projects and contribute to the foundation's mission.
