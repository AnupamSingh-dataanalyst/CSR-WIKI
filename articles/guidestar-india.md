# GuideStar India
GuideStar India is a free, online platform that provides detailed information about non-profit organisations in India, promoting transparency and accountability in the NGO sector. By registering on GuideStar India, NGOs can demonstrate their commitment to transparency and good governance, making it easier for donors, funders, and other stakeholders to make informed decisions. GuideStar India is a initiative of TechSoup India, in partnership with Civicus, and is supported by the NITI Aayog and the Ministry of Corporate Affairs.

## Key Details
GuideStar India has a database of over 10,000 NGOs, and provides access to information such as an NGO's mission, goals, activities, financial statements, and governance structure. NGOs can register on the platform by filling out a simple online form, and can also upload documents such as their registration certificate, annual reports, and audited financial statements. The platform is free for NGOs to use, and is accessible to anyone with an internet connection.

## Who Needs This
GuideStar India is relevant to all NGOs in India, regardless of their size or scope of work. However, it is particularly useful for NGOs that rely on donations or grants from external sources, as it provides a way for them to demonstrate their transparency and accountability to potential funders. Companies that are required to spend 2% of their net profits on corporate social responsibility (CSR) activities under the Companies Act, 2013, can also use GuideStar India to find and partner with NGOs that are working in areas aligned with their CSR goals.

## Step-by-Step Process
To register on GuideStar India, an NGO can follow these steps:
1. Visit the GuideStar India website and click on the "Register" button.
2. Fill out the online registration form, which will require basic information about the NGO such as its name, address, and contact details.
3. Upload required documents, such as the NGO's registration certificate and annual reports.
4. Verify the NGO's email address and phone number.

## Important Deadlines / Penalties
There are no specific deadlines or penalties associated with registering on GuideStar India, as it is a voluntary platform. However, NGOs that are registered on the platform are required to keep their information up-to-date, and to respond to any queries or requests for information from GuideStar India or its users.
