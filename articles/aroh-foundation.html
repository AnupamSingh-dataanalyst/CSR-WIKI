AROH Foundation

The AROH Foundation is a non-governmental organization (NGO) based in India that works towards the socio-economic development of underprivileged communities. Established with the aim of empowering marginalized sections of society, AROH focuses on education, healthcare, and livelihood generation. Through its initiatives, the foundation strives to create a positive impact on the lives of the disadvantaged, promoting equality and social justice.

Key Details

AROH Foundation was founded in 2001 and is registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860.
