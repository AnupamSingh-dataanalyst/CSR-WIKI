MCA CSR Portal

The MCA CSR Portal is an online platform introduced by the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) for companies to file their Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) reports and disclosures. This portal aims to bring transparency and accountability in CSR spending by companies in India. The portal is a part of the MCA's efforts to implement and monitor the CSR provisions under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013.

Key Details

The MCA CSR Portal requires companies to file their CSR reports in Form CSR-2, which includes details of CSR policy, projects, and expenditures. Companies with a net worth of Rs. 500 crore or more, or a turnover of Rs. 1,000 crore or more, or a net profit of Rs. 5 crore or more, are required to spend at least 2% of their average net profits on CSR activities. The portal also provides a unique CSR Registration Number to each company, which is used to track and verify their CSR filings.

Who Needs This

The MCA CSR Portal is applicable to all companies that come under the purview of Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, including Indian companies, foreign companies, and section 8 companies. NGOs and other organizations that receive CSR funds from companies are also required to be registered on the portal. This includes NGOs that are registered under Section 12A and 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961, and have a valid FCRA registration.

Step-by-Step Process

To file CSR reports on the MCA CSR Portal, companies need to follow these steps: 1. Register on the portal using their Corporate Identity Number (CIN) and other company details. 2. Obtain a unique CSR Registration Number. 3. Fill and submit Form CSR-2, which includes details of CSR policy, projects, and expenditures. 4. Upload supporting documents, such as CSR project reports and audited financial statements. 5. Verify and confirm the filing using a One-Time Password (OTP) sent to the company's registered email and mobile number.

Important Deadlines / Penalties

Companies are required to file their CSR reports on the MCA CSR Portal within 30 days of the end of the financial year. Failure to file CSR reports or non-compliance with CSR provisions can result in penalties, including fines and imprisonment. The penalty for non-compliance can range from Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 25 lakh, and/or imprisonment for up to 3 years.
