Humans For Humanity

Humans For Humanity is a non-governmental organization (NGO) that aims to provide humanitarian aid and support to underprivileged communities in India. The organization focuses on various initiatives, including education, healthcare, and environmental conservation, to create a positive impact on society. By leveraging resources and partnerships, Humans For Humanity strives to empower marginalized groups and promote sustainable development.

Key Details

Humans For Humanity is registered under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882, and is also registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013. The organization is required to file annual returns with the Registrar of Companies and submit audited financial statements to the Income Tax Department. To receive tax exemptions, Humans For Humanity must comply with the provisions of Section 12A and Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961, and file Form 10B with the Income Tax Department.

Who Needs This

Humans For Humanity is relevant to NGOs and companies that operate in the social impact space and are committed to corporate social responsibility (CSR). Under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, companies with a net worth of Rs. 500 crore or more, or a turnover of Rs. 1,000 crore or more, or a net profit of Rs. 5 crore or more, are required to spend at least 2% of their average net profits on CSR activities. Humans For Humanity can partner with such companies to implement CSR projects and initiatives.

To partner with Humans For Humanity, companies and NGOs can follow these steps: 1. Identify the areas of collaboration, such as education, healthcare, or environmental conservation. 2. Important Deadlines / Penalties

Companies that fail to comply with the CSR provisions under Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, may face penalties, including fines and imprisonment. The CSR expenditure must be incurred during the financial year, and any unspent amount must be transferred to a CSR fund or a registered trust. The deadline for filing the CSR report (Form CSR-2) with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs is within 30 days of the board meeting in which the CSR report is approved.
