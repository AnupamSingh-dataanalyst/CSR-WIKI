# Baranagore Ramakrishna Mission Ashrama High School
The Baranagore Ramakrishna Mission Ashrama High School is a renowned educational institution located in Baranagar, Kolkata, West Bengal, India. It was established in 1912 by Swami Premananda, a direct disciple of Sri Ramakrishna, with the objective of providing quality education to students while inculcating the values of the Ramakrishna Mission. The school is affiliated with the West Bengal Board of Secondary Education and has a long history of academic excellence.

## Key Details
The school offers classes from VI to XII, with a focus on both academic and extracurricular activities. It has a student strength of around 1,200, with a teacher-student ratio of 1:20. The school has a well-equipped infrastructure, including laboratories, a library, and a playground. Admission to the school is based on an entrance examination, and the annual fee is around Rs. 10,000. The school is recognized by the Department of School Education, Government of West Bengal, and is also registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860.

The filled-in form, along with the required documents and fee, must be submitted within the specified deadline. The school then conducts an entrance examination, followed by an interview for selected candidates. Once admitted, students can avail of the various facilities and participate in the school's activities.
