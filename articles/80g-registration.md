# 80G Registration
The 80G registration is a tax deduction certificate granted to non-governmental organizations (NGOs) in India, allowing donors to claim a deduction of 50% of the donated amount from their taxable income. This registration is issued under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961, and is a vital step for NGOs seeking to attract donations. By obtaining an 80G registration, NGOs can increase their credibility and encourage more donations.

## Key Details
To be eligible for 80G registration, an NGO must be registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, or the Indian Trusts Act, 1882, or as a Section 8 company under the Companies Act, 2013. The application for 80G registration is made in Form 10G, which is submitted to the Commissioner of Income Tax. The registration is typically valid for a period of 5 years, after which it needs to be renewed. The application fee for 80G registration is currently Rs. 5,000, and the processing time can take up to 6 months.

## Who Needs This
The 80G registration is applicable to all NGOs in India that are engaged in charitable activities and are seeking to attract donations. This includes trusts, societies, and Section 8 companies that are involved in activities such as education, healthcare, poverty alleviation, and environmental conservation. Companies that make donations to NGOs with 80G registration can also claim a deduction under Section 35AC of the Income Tax Act.

## Step-by-Step Process
To obtain an 80G registration, an NGO needs to follow these steps:
* Register under the relevant act (Societies Registration Act, Indian Trusts Act, or Companies Act)
* Prepare the necessary documents, including the trust deed, registration certificate, and audited financial statements
* Fill out Form 10G and submit it to the Commissioner of Income Tax
* Pay the application fee of Rs. 5,000
* Wait for the processing and approval of the application, which can take up to 6 months

## Important Deadlines / Penalties
The application for 80G registration should be made within 1 year from the date of registration of the NGO. If the application is not made within this timeframe, the NGO may lose out on potential donations. There are no specific penalties for not having an 80G registration, but NGOs without this registration may find it difficult to attract donations.
