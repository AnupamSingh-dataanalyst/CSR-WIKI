Bharat Sevashram Sangha

Bharat Sevashram Sangha is a Indian social service organization that aims to provide humanitarian aid and promote social welfare in India. Founded in 1917 by Acharya Swami Pranavananda, the organization has been working tirelessly to provide relief to the poor, the distressed, and the marginalized sections of society. With its headquarters in Kolkata, West Bengal, Bharat Sevashram Sangha has expanded its operations to various parts of India and abroad.

Key Details

Bharat Sevashram Sangha is registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, and is exempt from income tax under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The organization has a strong presence in over 100 locations across India, with a network of ashrams, schools, hospitals, and other social service institutions. Bharat Sevashram Sangha is also registered with the National Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) Hub, which enables companies to contribute to the organization's social welfare activities.

Who Needs This

Bharat Sevashram Sangha's services and programs are available to all sections of society, particularly the underprivileged and marginalized communities. NGOs and companies involved in social welfare activities, such as education, healthcare, and poverty alleviation, can partner with Bharat Sevashram Sangha to amplify their impact. Additionally, individuals and organizations looking to contribute to CSR initiatives can support Bharat Sevashram Sangha's activities, which are aligned with the Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013.
