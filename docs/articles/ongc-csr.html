ONGC CSR

ONGC CSR refers to the corporate social responsibility initiatives undertaken by Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC), a public sector undertaking (PSU) in India. As one of the largest PSU companies in the country, ONGC has a significant CSR budget and implements various programs to contribute to the social and economic development of the communities it operates in. ONGC's CSR initiatives are guided by the provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, and the CSR Rules, 2014.

Key Details

ONGC's CSR policy is based on the principles of sustainability, transparency, and accountability, with a focus on education, healthcare, and community development. The company has a dedicated CSR department that oversees the implementation of its CSR programs, which include initiatives such as the ONGC Scholarship Scheme, the ONGC Super 30 program, and the ONGC Health Camps. ONGC's CSR expenditure is around 2% of its average net profit for the last three financial years, as per the requirements of Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013. The company submits its CSR report in Form CSR-2 to the Ministry of Corporate Affairs through the MCA21 portal.

Who Needs This

ONGC's CSR programs are open to NGOs, educational institutions, and community organizations that work in the areas of education, healthcare, and community development. To be eligible for ONGC's CSR funding, NGOs must be registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, or the Indian Trusts Act, 1882, and have a valid FCRA registration if they receive foreign funding. Companies that are looking to partner with ONGC for CSR initiatives must have a good track record of CSR implementation and must align their CSR goals with ONGC's CSR policy.

Step-by-Step Process

To apply for ONGC's CSR funding, NGOs and companies must follow these steps: 1. Review ONGC's CSR policy and guidelines to ensure alignment with their own goals and objectives. 2. Submit a proposal outlining their project plan, budget, and expected outcomes. 3. Provide all necessary documents, including registration certificates, audited financial statements, and FCRA registration (if applicable). 4. Wait for the review and approval of their proposal by ONGC's CSR department.

Important Deadlines / Penalties

ONGC's CSR department typically invites proposals for CSR funding on a quarterly basis, with deadlines for submission usually falling on the 15th of March, June, September, and December. NGOs and companies that fail to submit their proposals on time may miss the opportunity for funding. Additionally, companies that fail to spend the required 2% of their average net profit on CSR activities may face penalties, including a fine of up to Rs. 25 lakhs and imprisonment of up to three years for the company's directors.
