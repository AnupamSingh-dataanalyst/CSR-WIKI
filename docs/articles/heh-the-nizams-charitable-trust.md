# H.E.H. the Nizam's Charitable Trust
H.E.H. the Nizam's Charitable Trust is a charitable trust established in 1953 by the last Nizam of Hyderabad, Mir Osman Ali Khan. The trust was created to utilize a portion of the Nizam's private funds for charitable purposes, focusing on education, healthcare, and other social welfare activities. The trust operates under the Indian Trusts Act, 1882, and is registered with the Income Tax Department, Government of India.

## Key Details
The H.E.H. the Nizam's Charitable Trust has a corpus fund of approximately Rs. 30 crores, which generates an annual income of around Rs. 10-12 crores. The trust provides financial assistance to various institutions, organizations, and individuals for charitable purposes, with a focus on the states of Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and Maharashtra. The trust is managed by a board of trustees, which includes representatives from the Nizam's family, government officials, and other prominent citizens.

## Who Needs This
The H.E.H. the Nizam's Charitable Trust is relevant to NGOs, educational institutions, hospitals, and other organizations involved in social welfare activities in the states of Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and Maharashtra. Companies operating in these states may also be interested in the trust's activities, particularly those with corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives focused on education, healthcare, and other social causes.

## Step-by-Step Process
To apply for financial assistance from the H.E.H. the Nizam's Charitable Trust, organizations and individuals must submit a detailed proposal outlining their project or activity, including its objectives, budget, and expected outcomes. The proposal must be submitted in the prescribed format, which can be obtained from the trust's office or website. The trust's board of trustees reviews the proposals and selects the ones that align with the trust's objectives and criteria.

## Important Deadlines / Penalties
The H.E.H. the Nizam's Charitable Trust typically invites applications for financial assistance during the months of June and December every year. Organizations and individuals must submit their proposals within the specified deadlines to be considered for funding. Failure to comply with the trust's guidelines and deadlines may result in rejection of the proposal.
