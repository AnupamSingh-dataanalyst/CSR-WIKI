# FCRA Registration
The Foreign Contribution Regulation Act (FCRA) registration is a mandatory requirement for non-governmental organizations (NGOs) in India that wish to receive foreign contributions. This registration is governed by the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) and is essential for NGOs to receive and utilize foreign funds. The FCRA registration ensures that foreign contributions are utilized for legitimate purposes and are not used to compromise the national interest.

## Key Details
The FCRA registration is granted for a period of 5 years, after which it needs to be renewed. To be eligible for FCRA registration, an NGO must have been in existence for at least 3 years and have spent at least Rs. 10 lakhs on its core activities during the last 3 years. The application for FCRA registration is made online through Form FC-3 on the FCRA website. The registration fee is Rs. 7,500 for new registrations and Rs. 5,000 for renewals.

## Who Needs This
FCRA registration is required for all NGOs, including charities, trusts, societies, and section 8 companies, that intend to receive foreign contributions. This includes organizations that receive funds from foreign governments, international organizations, foreign companies, and even Indian citizens living abroad. However, NGOs that are exempt from FCRA registration include those that are exempt under the FCRA, 2010, such as government-funded organizations and organizations that do not receive foreign contributions.

## Step-by-Step Process
To obtain FCRA registration, an NGO needs to follow these steps:
1. Ensure that the NGO is registered under an existing law, such as the Societies Registration Act, 1860, or the Indian Trusts Act, 1882.
2. Open a separate bank account exclusively for foreign contributions.
3. Apply online through Form FC-3 on the FCRA website.
4. Upload the required documents, including the NGO's registration certificate, Memorandum of Association, and audited financial statements.
5. Pay the registration fee of Rs. 7,500.

## Important Deadlines / Penalties
The application for FCRA registration must be made at least 6 months prior to the expected date of receipt of foreign contribution. If an NGO fails to register under the FCRA, it may face penalties, including a fine of up to Rs. 1 lakh and imprisonment of up to 3 years. Additionally, if an NGO fails to renew its FCRA registration, it may face a penalty of Rs. 10,000 per day.
