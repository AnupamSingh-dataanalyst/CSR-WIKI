# Reliance Foundation CSR
Reliance Foundation is the philanthropic arm of Reliance Industries Limited, one of India's largest private sector companies. Through Reliance Foundation, the company undertakes its corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives, focusing on areas such as education, healthcare, rural transformation, and sports for development. Reliance Foundation's CSR efforts are guided by the provisions of Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, and the Companies (Corporate Social Responsibility Policy) Rules, 2014.

## Key Details
Reliance Foundation's CSR initiatives are implemented in accordance with the CSR policy of Reliance Industries Limited, which is approved by the company's Board of Directors. The company spends at least 2% of its average net profits made during the three immediately preceding financial years on CSR activities, as mandated by the Companies Act, 2013. Reliance Foundation's CSR projects are implemented through partnerships with NGOs, community organizations, and government agencies, and the foundation also provides funding support to NGOs working in areas aligned with its CSR focus areas.

## Who Needs This
Reliance Foundation's CSR initiatives are relevant to NGOs and community organizations working in the areas of education, healthcare, rural transformation, and sports for development. Companies looking to partner with Reliance Foundation for CSR initiatives must have a strong track record of implementing projects in these areas and must be registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, or be a society or trust registered under the relevant state laws. NGOs and companies can apply for funding support or partnership opportunities with Reliance Foundation by submitting a proposal outlining their project plan, budget, and expected outcomes.

## Step-by-Step Process
To apply for funding support or partnership opportunities with Reliance Foundation, NGOs and companies can follow these steps:
1. Review Reliance Foundation's CSR focus areas and ensure that their project aligns with these areas.
2. Prepare a detailed project proposal outlining the project plan, budget, and expected outcomes.
3. Submit the proposal to Reliance Foundation through their website or by email.
4. Reliance Foundation's CSR team will review the proposal and may request additional information or clarification.
5. If the proposal is approved, Reliance Foundation will work with the NGO or company to finalize the partnership agreement and provide funding support.

## Important Deadlines / Penalties
Reliance Foundation typically accepts proposals for CSR partnerships on a rolling basis, but NGOs and companies are advised to submit their proposals at least 6-8 months prior to the proposed project start date. There are no specific penalties for NGOs and companies that apply for CSR partnerships with Reliance Foundation, but the foundation may reject proposals that do not align with its CSR focus areas or that do not meet its partnership criteria.
