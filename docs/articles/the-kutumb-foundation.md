# The Kutumb Foundation
The Kutumb Foundation is a non-governmental organization (NGO) based in India, focused on providing education and skill development opportunities to underprivileged children and youth. Established with the goal of empowering marginalized communities, the foundation works towards creating a more equitable society through its various programs and initiatives. By leveraging partnerships with corporations, governments, and individuals, The Kutumb Foundation aims to drive positive change and improve the lives of thousands of beneficiaries across the country.

## Key Details
The Kutumb Foundation operates primarily in the National Capital Region (NCR) of Delhi, with a specific focus on education, vocational training, and community development. The foundation's programs are designed to cater to the unique needs of its target audience, including children from economically disadvantaged backgrounds, dropouts, and those at risk of exploitation. Key initiatives include the establishment of learning centers, provision of scholarships, and implementation of vocational training modules to enhance employability.

## Who Needs This
The Kutumb Foundation's services and programs are geared towards NGOs, community organizations, and corporate social responsibility (CSR) initiatives that aim to make a positive impact on the lives of underprivileged children and youth. Companies operating in India, particularly those mandated to spend 2% of their net profits on CSR activities under the Companies Act, 2013, may find The Kutumb Foundation to be a suitable partner for their social responsibility endeavors. Additionally, NGOs and community groups seeking to collaborate or receive support for their education and skill development projects can benefit from engaging with The Kutumb Foundation.
