CSR MOU Agreement

A CSR MOU Agreement, or Memorandum of Understanding, is a formal document that outlines the terms and conditions of a partnership between a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) and a company undertaking Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives in India. This agreement is essential for ensuring clarity and transparency in the collaboration, including the objectives, roles, and responsibilities of both parties. By signing a CSR MOU Agreement, NGOs and CSR companies can establish a mutually beneficial and legally binding partnership.

Key Details

The CSR MOU Agreement typically includes specifics such as the project duration, budget, and implementation timeline. According to the Companies Act, 2013, and the CSR Rules, 2014, companies with a net worth of Rs. 500 crore or more, or a turnover of Rs. 1,000 crore or more, or a net profit of Rs. 5 crore or more, are required to spend at least 2% of their average net profits on CSR activities. The agreement should also mention the CSR activities to be undertaken, as specified in Schedule VII of the Companies Act, 2013, such as education, healthcare, and environmental sustainability. Additionally, the agreement may include details on reporting requirements, monitoring, and evaluation, as well as the roles and responsibilities of the NGO and the CSR company.

Who Needs This

This agreement applies to NGOs and CSR companies that intend to collaborate on CSR projects in India. NGOs registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860, the Indian Trusts Act, 1882, or the Companies Act, 2013, and having a valid FCRA registration, if applicable, can partner with CSR companies. Companies that meet the CSR eligibility criteria, as specified in Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013, can undertake CSR initiatives in collaboration with NGOs.

Step-by-Step Process

To establish a CSR MOU Agreement, the following steps can be taken: 1. Identify the CSR project and objectives. 2. Select a suitable NGO partner. 3. Draft the MOU agreement, including the terms and conditions, roles, and responsibilities. 4. Negotiate and finalize the agreement. 5. Sign the agreement, ensuring that it is duly stamped and witnessed. 6. Register the agreement, if required, on the MCA21 portal or with the relevant state authorities.

Important Deadlines / Penalties

The CSR MOU Agreement should be signed and implemented within the financial year, as the CSR expenditure is required to be made within the same year. Failure to comply with the CSR provisions, including the MOU agreement, may result in penalties, such as a fine of up to Rs. 1 crore, as specified in Section 135 of the Companies Act, 2013.
